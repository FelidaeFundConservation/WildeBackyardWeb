Expand Wildlife Research to Your Backyard

Felidae has studied Bay Area wild cats for over 15 years, cataloguing more than 6 million images of terrestrial mammals. With 158 cameras currently in the field, the Wilde Backyard program expands this research to residential properties, generating new coverage across the complete Bay Area landscape while building community stewardship and engagement.

Citizen Science Through Mobile Technology

Record wildlife sightings using your mobile device. Wilde Backyard uses AI to process images and generate rich datasets of urban wildlife. Residents, hikers, bikers, and trail users can contribute to regional data collection, supporting Felidae's research and collaborative conservation projects across disciplines.
